### FAB : Fabrication Facility 

It's literally just a multi billion $$$ where computer chips: ***Semiconductors*** are made. 
---
## Size of the Factory : 300mm VS 200mm

Depending on the size of the wafer factories either use 300mm or 200mm Fabs.

- 200 mm (8 inches)
- 300 mm (12 inches) : Bigger, fit more chips, but huge and heavy, thus factories are automated for transport *without* us HUMANS
---
## What is a LOT

Group of Semiconductor wafers (usually 25 wafers) which are TRACKED. PROCESSED. TESTED together, as a singular unit in the FAB facility.

* Basically, engineers wanted to track every single wafer one by onebut with millions, it will complicate things. *

* thus use batch called a LOT (25 wafers) *
