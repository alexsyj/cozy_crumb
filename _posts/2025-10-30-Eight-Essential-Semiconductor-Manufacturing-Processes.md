### What is a semiconductor?

Before we get into semiconductors we have to first know these two first:

- Conductor: Electricity basically flows
- Insulator: Electricity doesn't flow.

Semiconductor on the other hand acts as both conductor and insulator depending on the conditions. eg. Silicon

### Eight Essential Semiconductor Manufacturing Processes

---

## 1. Wafer Fabrication

Raw silicon is melted into cylinders, sliced thin, and polished into discs

---

## 2. Oxidation

Apply high heat. Literally baking in order to create an insulation layer to stop electrical LEAKS.

---

## 3. Photolithography

Print micro designs on the surface by shooting light sensitive liquid called Photoresist with UV/EUV light through a stencil. 

---

## 4. Etching

Use gas phase reactive plasmas to carve the channels. Deep trenches are carved

---

## 5. Deposition and Ion implantation

- Deposit insulating films on to the surface by spraying.
- Shoot the wafer with chemical impurities such as boron or phosphorus.

Thus, giving the silicon the ability to switch between blocking and conducting electricity!

---

## 6. Metal wiring

Lay down copper or Aluminum micro wires to link billions of isolated switches together.

---

## 7. EDS (Electrical die sorting)

Test the raw wafer sheet to seperate working dies from defective ones.

---

## 8. Packaging

slice the wafer into individual chips, then seal them inside protective black, dark, plastic casing.
